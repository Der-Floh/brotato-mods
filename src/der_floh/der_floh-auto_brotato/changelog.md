# Changelog

## [1.0.0] – 2026-11-24

### Added

- Recycles unwanted crate items and takes wanted ones, chosen per item and, with Abyssal Terrors, by curse; also the extra items of a Treasure Map or a Pearl.
- Picks level-ups by weight.
- Starts the next wave from the shop, after an optional countdown that Esc cancels, up to a wave you choose.
- Lists items, weapons and level-ups from the game itself, so content from updates, DLCs and other mods shows up by itself.
- Settings pages in the pause menu and on the title screen, and the switches in dami's and Oudstand's Mod Options.
